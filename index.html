DESIGNED AS A BANK CONTACT-CENTER CONCEPT

A calmer front door.
A responsible next step.

Built to be explored by cardholders, challenged by reviewers, and shaped with bank partners.

The proposed product is licensed to each bank or contact center. Any live identity checks, card actions, security controls, and human handoff would need that bank’s approval and integration.

FUTURE SERVICE CONCEPT

A borrowed-phone hotline could be explored as a future bank-approved channel. This browser demo places no calls and connects to no bank.

Before you try

Use fictional information only. Do not enter real PINs, OTPs, national IDs, full card numbers, addresses, or bank credentials. The app keeps the conversation in page memory only, with no app backend or browser storage. Reset clears it; changing language starts a new session. Browser speech services may process audio under their own policies.
